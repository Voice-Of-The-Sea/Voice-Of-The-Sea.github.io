Why are there Send OR Print Options (this is a little confusing)

We offer three ways to receive your order.

  1. Complete the order form and use the Send button. This is preferred as it guarantees that all the information we need is provided. Mail your check or money order seperately.
  2. Complete the order form and use the Print button. Attach your check or money order and mail. This does not guarantee that all the required fields are completed.
  3. Do not complete the form, use the Print Button, and complete in pen. Attach your check or money order and mail.

As a small business, we are unable to accept credit cards at this time, which means that we must receive your check or money order through the mail regardless of which ordering option best fits your needs.

We realize this may be a little cumbersome, however, we feel that you will agree that our product was worth the effort.
